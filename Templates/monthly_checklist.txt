Monthly Accounting Checklist

Month:

[ ] Download Revolut Business bank statement.
[ ] Save all purchase receipts and screenshots.
[ ] Save all sales/platform reports.
[ ] Save shipping receipts.
[ ] Enter purchases in Purchases sheet.
[ ] Enter sales in Sales_Log sheet.
[ ] Enter expenses in Expenses sheet.
[ ] Enter owner investments/private withdrawals in Owner_Money sheet.
[ ] Check Dashboard net profit.
[ ] Check yearly turnover against VAT threshold.
[ ] Set aside 30-40% of profit as tax buffer.
[ ] Back up the Excel file and receipt folder.
