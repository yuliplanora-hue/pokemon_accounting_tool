Suggested Card Condition Policy

Mint:
Perfect or nearly perfect card. Use carefully.

Near Mint:
Clean front and back, no major whitening, scratches or dents.

Excellent:
Minor edge/corner whitening or small surface marks, still very collectible.

Good:
Visible wear but no major damage.

Light Played:
More visible wear, scratches or whitening.

Played:
Clear wear, bends, scratches or multiple visible defects.

Poor/Damaged:
Creases, water damage, heavy wear, peeling, ink, or other major defects.

Vendor advice:
Always describe condition conservatively. Photos are part of the description.
