Pokémon / TCG Toiminimi Accounting Toolkit - No VAT Version

Use this toolkit if:
- You have a Finnish toiminimi / private trader.
- You are not VAT registered yet.
- You buy and sell Pokémon / TCG cards and sealed products.
- You want to track inventory, purchases, sales, expenses, profit and owner money movements.

Main rule:
Since you are NOT VAT registered, record all amounts VAT included.
Do not add VAT to your sales.
Do not deduct VAT from purchases.

Recommended routine:
1. Use Revolut Business only for business income and expenses.
2. Enter every purchase in the Excel Purchases sheet.
3. Enter every sale in Sales_Log.
4. Save all receipts/screenshots/payment proofs in the folder structure.
5. Review Monthly_Summary once per month.
6. Monitor yearly turnover against the VAT threshold.
7. Use Year_End_Form5 as preparation for Form 5, but verify before filing.
